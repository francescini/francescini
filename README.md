# Francesca Gomez

Founder of [Wiser Human](https://www.wiserhuman.ai), an AI safety organisation. I research how AI agents behave when their task conflicts with ethical, legal or safety constraints, and how the environment they work in can make the safer path the easier one.

- **Agent Escalation Line (beta):** a safety service AI agents can escalate to when their task conflicts with their rules. If you deploy agents, setup takes a few minutes: [wiser-human/agent-escalation-line](https://github.com/wiser-human/agent-escalation-line)
- **Research:** [arXiv 2510.05192](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05192), [arXiv 2608.29460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29460)
- **Writing:** [blog.wiserhuman.ai](https://blog.wiserhuman.ai)
